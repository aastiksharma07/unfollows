See who doesn’t follow you back.

Drop in your Instagram export. Nothing leaves your device.

How to get these files
  1. In Instagram, open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Some of your information → Followers and following.
  3. Pick Download to device, format JSON, date range All time.
  4. Unzip the download and open connections/followers_and_following.
  5. Upload followers_1.json (and any followers_2.json…) and following.json here.
